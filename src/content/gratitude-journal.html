What it is

The cover titles the work The Ultimate Gratitude Journal and the subtitle “66 Days Action Plan Based on NLP”. The opening note, written by Sampath Kumar, invites the reader into a 66-day gratitude practice and talks about choice during uncertain times.

A later page refers to a related conversation called “Heal Your Mind to Heal Your Life”. This website does not have a separate public syllabus for that title, so it is mentioned only as a reference inside the journal.

What we will not promise

The journal’s own introduction uses hopeful language about abundance. This website does not turn that into a guarantee. Gratitude practice is educational. It is not medical care, psychotherapy, or a treatment for any condition.
