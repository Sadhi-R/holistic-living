From the workshop

When the inner life changes, life changes.

Outer success can still leave people unsatisfied when the inner life is unsettled. Thoughts lead to emotions, emotions to actions, and actions to results.

This is an educational session, not therapy and not a promise of a particular outcome.

About the mentor

Five areas, one root

The workshop names the places people feel stuck.

These themes treat inner life as the root beneath everyday pressure.

Health

Stress, sleep, and energy.

Relationships

Misunderstandings, conflict, and distance.

Money

The feeling of insecurity that can remain even after earning.

Business

Pressure, fear of failure, and overthinking.

Career

Confusion, lack of clarity, and burnout.

What a session covers

Ten areas of practice.

These are the agenda themes participants work with. They are not guaranteed results.

  1. Noticing negative thoughts and working with them
  2. Practices intended to calm anxiety and stress
  3. Emotional mastery: relating to emotions with more skill
  4. Interrupting overthinking
  5. Recognising limiting beliefs
  6. Building a growth mindset
  7. Setting clearer goals and an action plan
  8. Clearer communication in relationships
  9. Self-confidence and inner peace as practice, not a promise
  10. A daily routine for continuing the work
