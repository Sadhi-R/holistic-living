What the workshop is

Neuro-linguistic programming is presented here as a practical way to understand how mind, language, and patterns work together. Holistic living, in the same materials, means looking at body, mind, emotions, relationships, and work as one life rather than separate problems.

The chain used in the brochure is simple: inner thoughts, then emotions, then actions, then results.

What the agenda covers

  1. Noticing negative thoughts and working with them
  2. Practices intended to calm anxiety and stress
  3. Emotional mastery: relating to emotions with more skill
  4. Interrupting overthinking
  5. Recognising limiting beliefs
  6. Building a growth mindset
  7. Setting clearer goals and an action plan
  8. Clearer communication in relationships
  9. Self-confidence and inner peace as practice, not a promise
  10. A daily routine for continuing the work

Who it is for

The brochure invites

  • Working professionals. People looking for steadier balance under job pressure.
  • Business owners. People who want clearer, more confident decisions.
