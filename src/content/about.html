How the work is described

Workshop posters and the Holistic Living brochure introduce Sampath Kumar as a Growth Mindset Mentor. The sessions are described as NLP-based: a practical look at how mind, language, and patterns work together, and how a person can notice unhelpful patterns and practise more constructive ones.

Holistic living, in these materials, means seeing life as a whole — body, mind, emotions, relationships, and work — rather than as separate compartments. The brochure connects that inner life with health, relationships, money, business, and career.

What this page does not claim

The brochure also uses the words “NLP practitioner”, “psychologist”, and “1000+ people trained”. Those statements are not published on this website until they are confirmed for public use.
