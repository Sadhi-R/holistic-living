How the work is described

Holistic Living materials introduce Sampath Kumar as a Growth Mindset Mentor. The sessions are NLP-based: a practical look at how mind, language, and patterns work together, and how a person can notice unhelpful patterns and practise more constructive ones.

Holistic living means seeing life as a whole — body, mind, emotions, relationships, and work — rather than as separate compartments. The work connects that inner life with health, relationships, money, business, and career.
